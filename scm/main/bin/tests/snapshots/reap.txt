browse reap — Clean up sessions whose launcher has died or gone stale

USAGE:
    browse reap [--dry-run] [--max-age <DURATION>]

Closes recorded sessions whose launching process no longer exists. With --max-age it also closes sessions older than that even if their launcher is alive. Takes no --port: it looks at every recorded session.

OPTIONS:
    --dry-run                List what would be reaped without closing or deleting anything
    --max-age <DURATION>     Also reap sessions older than this: seconds, or 30s, 5m, 1h
    -h, --help               Print this help

OUTPUT:
    One line per session reaped (or "Would reap" with --dry-run) and a total, or "No orphaned or stale sessions found."

EXAMPLES:
    browse reap --dry-run
    browse reap --max-age 1h

EXIT CODES: 0 success, 1 action failed, 2 invalid usage, 3 timeout, 4 browser unreachable
